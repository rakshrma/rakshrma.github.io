Rakesh Sharma

PhD Student in Bioengineering · University of Pennsylvania

I am a PhD student in Bioengineering at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Prof. Walter R. T. Witschey and Prof. Victoria M. Gershuni. I build clinically deployable multimodal AI—vision–language models, multi-agent systems, and imaging pipelines—for gastrointestinal disorders and post-surgical complication prediction. My recent first-author work includes clinically-aware evaluation of medical vision–language models. I serve as a peer reviewer for Nature Communications, the Korean Journal of Radiology, the Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine, ACM-BCB, and SIIM-CAIMI.

Before my PhD, I spent four years as a data scientist at Eli Lilly working on drug-delivery devices and imaging pipelines, co-founded a surgical robotics company as CTO, developed microfluidic diagnostic platforms at Achira Labs, and built computer-vision systems at Wipro. I am an inventor on three patent applications and hold a B.Tech–M.Tech in Bioengineering from IIT (BHU) Varanasi.
